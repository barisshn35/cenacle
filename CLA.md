organization tells us when someone should be removed.

## How to sign

In your first merge request, add a line to [`cla/signatures.txt`](cla/signatures.txt):

```text
<your commit e-mail>  <Your-Name>  <YYYY-MM-DD>  individual
```

Adding that line in a signed-off commit (`git commit -s`) means you accept this
agreement for all your past and future Contributions to Cenacle. CI checks every commit
of a merge request: signed off (DCO), by an e-mail address listed there.
