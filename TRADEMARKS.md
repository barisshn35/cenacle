The code is open source (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)); the names and the logo are not part of
that license. This policy says how you may use them, so that people can tell the
original project from other software.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your software works with, runs on or was built from Cenacle, in plain words
  ("chat for our team, based on Cenacle").
- Run, modify and redistribute Cenacle under its license, keeping the name in notices,
  the about screen and the documentation.
- Write about Cenacle, review it, teach it and show its logo unchanged next to that text.
- Use the name for user groups, meetups and events about Cenacle that are free to attend,
  as long as it is clear they are not run by the project.

## You need written permission to

- Distribute a **modified** version under the name "Cenacle" or with its logo, or offer
  it as a service under that name. Modified versions must carry a different name and
  logo (you may say "based on Cenacle").
- Use the name or logo in the name of a company, product, service or domain name.
- Sell merchandise with the name or logo.
- Suggest that the project endorses, sponsors or certifies you or your offer.

## Never

- Change the logo, combine it with your marks or use look-alike names and logos.
- Use the marks in a way that misleads people about who made or supports something.

Ask for permission or report misuse with a confidential issue in the project's issue
tracker. This policy may change; the current version is in the repository.
